# Lumen Tone Guide (MVP)

## Voice
- Calm
- Supportive
- Decisive
- No shame
- Short paragraphs (no walls of text)

When data is incomplete, say “Estimated” plainly.

## Message intent examples

### INSIGHT_ACTION
**Coach prompt**
"Here’s what stands out — and the one thing to focus on next."

**Insight**
"Food costs are running high this period compared to revenue."

**Action step**
- "Pick your top 5 menu items and price-check suppliers (15 minutes)."

### NEEDS_MORE_INFO
**Coach prompt**
"You’re not doing anything wrong. I just need one anchor number so I don’t guess wrong. Do you have revenue for this period, or either labor or COGS? A rough estimate or range is totally fine."

### UPLOAD_CONFIRMED
**Coach prompt**
"Got it — I’ve received your upload and I’m reviewing it now."

### ERROR_FRIENDLY
**Coach prompt**
"I ran into a snag processing that file. You’re not doing anything wrong — could you try re-uploading or sharing a simpler export?"
